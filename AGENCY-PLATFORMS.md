# Agency platforms: GoHighLevel and its alternatives

Researched 2026-08-01. **We are an agency** - we sell to contractors, we are not
the contractor. So agency-tier platforms are the relevant category, unlike Podium
which is sold direct to the business owner.

**Standing decision: buy nothing until client #1 pays.** This exists so the
comparison is ready when that happens, not to justify a purchase now.

## GoHighLevel, correctly

Direct from gohighlevel.com/pricing.

| Plan | Monthly | Sub-accounts | What you actually get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $97 | 3 | Core CRM, funnels, automation. Internal agency use. |
| Unlimited | $297 | Unlimited | Agency branding, custom domain, rebill phone/email **at cost, no markup** |
| Agency Pro | $497 | Unlimited | **SaaS Mode** - automated sub-account creation, rebill **with markup** |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | - | White-label mobile app, HIPAA |

**Correction to something stated earlier in this project: the "$97 gets you
white-label reselling" framing is wrong.**

- $97 gets the software for internal use with 3 sub-accounts and no real reseller
  mechanics.
- $297 gets branding and unlimited sub-accounts, but rebilling is **pass-through
  only, no markup**.
- **$497 Agency Pro is the tier that actually lets you resell under your own
  brand with markup.** That is the real number for a reselling model.

This makes GoHighLevel *worse* for us than previously described, not better. The
build-it-yourself path at ~$40-57/month total (`LEAD-DELIVERY.md`) is compared
against $497/month, not $97.

## Alternatives that actually support reselling

| Platform | Entry | White-label / resell | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Centripe** | $99 | Yes, at $99 | $299 tier does roughly what GHL charges $497 for. New 2026 entrant. |
| **DashClicks** | $199 | Yes, unlimited sub-accounts | "Keep 100% of profits" model, no revenue share |
| **Vendasta** | $99 | Tiered - full white-label at Professional+ | Also resells 250+ third-party products wholesale |
| **SuiteDash** | $19 | Yes, "extreme white labeling" | No SMS/calling |
| Keap | $299 | **No** | Direct-to-business only |
| Close.com | $19/user | **No** | Sales CRM, not a reseller platform |
| ActiveCampaign | ~$15 | Enterprise only, unconfirmed | Pricing page is JS-rendered, could not verify |
| Zoho One | ~$37/user | **Not applicable** | Internal business suite, not a reseller platform |

**Authority caveat:** Centripe and DashClicks are new, low-adoption products
making claims about themselves. GoHighLevel and Vendasta are the established
players. Do not pick a platform on a marketing page alone.

## Cheaper Podium alternatives (if a client asks)

| Vendor | Price | Agency program? |
|---|---|---|
| **NiceJob** | **$75/mo** | Yes, explicit agency + affiliate program |
| **GatherUp** | **$99/mo** | Yes, white-label reputation management for agencies |
| Birdeye | Contact sales | Yes, explicit reseller program |
| Signpost | $199-749/mo + $199 setup | Not surfaced |
| Broadly | $799/mo | Not surfaced |
| Podium | Contact sales only | N/A |

**NiceJob at $75 and GatherUp at $99 are the only two with real public pricing
under $100 that also run agency programs.** Those are the names worth knowing.

**Stale-data trap:** Signpost and Broadly both moved upmarket into AI-receptionist
territory ($199-799/month). Any older blog citing "Signpost ~$50/mo" is out of
date.

**Podium publishes no pricing at all** - confirmed by fetching their page. Every
specific Podium dollar figure anywhere, including the $450-600 in our own
`MARKET-PRICING.md`, is a third-party estimate. Present it as an estimate.

## The recommendation

**Build, do not buy, at our current size.**

- DIY: ~$40-57/month total across all clients
- GoHighLevel Agency Pro: $497/month before a single client pays
- Centripe: $299/month for equivalent reseller mechanics

At 2 unpaid prospects, every one of these is a bet against ourselves. The DIY
stack does what we would actually use them for.

**Revisit when:** we pass ~10 clients (DIY hits $80-150/month and the operational
overhead grows), or a client needs something genuinely platform-shaped like a
full CRM with pipeline management.

**For Brandon:** GoHighLevel is not wrong, it is just premature and more
expensive than the videos imply. The reselling tier he has seen demoed is $497,
not $97.
