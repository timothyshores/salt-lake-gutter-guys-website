## Before the call

## What to point at on the demo

Lead with the reviews section. Every review on the page is a real one pulled
from his actual Yelp and Birdeye pages, and each one links out to the source so
he can click and verify it himself. Most cheap contractor websites use fake or
unsourced testimonials. That is the single most credible thing on his page.

Second, the call button. It sticks to the bottom of the screen on mobile the
whole time someone is scrolling. Most of his customers are finding him on a
phone during or right after a storm.

Third, the form. Fill it in on the call so he sees it actually deliver.

## If he pushes back on price

He is comparing this to nothing, to a Facebook page, or to building it himself.
Not to a $2500 agency site. The reviews are already live and verifiable, the
site works on a phone, and it captures leads the day it launches.

If he leans on the review count being small, that is fair. The honest answer:
these are the ones we could verify right now, the site texts you every new
lead, and the count grows from here. We can also get his Google Business
Profile set up so the review count actually builds.

## Questions to get answered on the call

Ordered by what unblocks the most. Get through as many as he has patience for,
1 through 4 are the ones that matter most.

1. **Where should new leads go - what email or cell number?**
   The contact form needs somewhere to deliver. Right now it routes to us.

2. **Any photos on your phone from recent jobs? Even bad phone-camera ones.**
   The job photos on the site are stock right now. Real ones are the single
   fastest visible upgrade and cost nothing.

3. **What address do you want listed?**
   Directories disagree - Yelp shows a 84116 zip, others show 2187 W 2200 S in
   West Valley City. Need his answer before we publish one.

4. **Is your contractor license current, and do you want "Licensed and Insured"
   on the site?**
   Ask it exactly that way. Public records show a license that expired
   11/30/2023. We are not putting that claim on the page until he confirms it,
   because a false one is a real liability for him.

5. **Do you carry general liability insurance? Who is the carrier?**
   Homeowners ask. If he has it, we display it. Ask for a certificate.

6. **Are you bonded? Do you carry workers comp?**
   Bonded plus insured plus licensed is the standard trust cluster local
   contractors put on their sites. Any of the three we can confirm, we display.

7. **How many years have you been doing this?**
   Every competitor leads with this. Ace Gutter says "since 1998." Right now
   his site says nothing.

8. **How far out do you travel? Whole Salt Lake Valley, or tighter?**
   Sets the service area copy.

9. **Do you have a Google Business Profile set up?**
   This is the follow-up sale. A site without a Google profile misses the
   highest-intent local searches, and it is how his review count actually
   grows.
